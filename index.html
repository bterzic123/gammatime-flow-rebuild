What this is

We opened GammaTime the way a new viewer does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at GammaTime: installed the app, watched the free episodes on gammatime.live until the paywall, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

Your app blocks screen recording, which is good for your content and hard for an audit. So the in-app screens are rebuilt from your App Store screenshots, and the paywall from the one on gammatime.live.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is built from yours: your black, your white Watch Now button, your Suggested for you sheet, your posters, your violet paywall with the gold BEST VALUE card. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
